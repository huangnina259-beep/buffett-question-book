    Ask in English or Chinese. Answers are in English, drawing on selected passages from Buffett’s letters, meetings and interviews. Munger’s remarks retain their own attribution.

    How an answer is prepared

    The service retrieves passages, drafts an explanation and checks its support. Open the numbered citations to read the original context.

    This is an experiment. Testing found omitted qualifications and failed requests. A model check is not human verification or a review of everything Buffett has said. There is no current-market feed.

    Your questions and account

    Generation uses your signed-in Codex account and allowance. Your question, limited conversation history and selected public passages are sent to its model service. Answers may take several minutes.

    The conversation stays in this page’s memory and clears on reload. The server keeps jobs in memory; the Codex runtime and model service have their own operational records and data handling.
